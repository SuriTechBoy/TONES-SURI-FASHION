## SHIPPING AND DELIVERY
URL: https://www.tonesfashion.com/policies/shipping-policy
Status: VERIFIED
- Shipping is subject to stock availability; the policy notes possible stock discrepancies.
- Flat shipping charge stated as Rs. 99 for orders below Rs. 1,498.
- Domestic transit generally stated as 2–10 days during normal sale periods.
- International transit generally stated as 4–22 days, depending on courier selection.
- Orders are usually dispatched within 2 business days of payment.
- Delivery address can be changed before dispatch.
- Out-of-stock items may be cancelled/refunded while the rest of the order is dispatched.
- Tracking link is provided after dispatch.
- Damaged-in-transit parcels should be addressed with customer service; the policy describes rejection/claim handling.
- Cancellation is accepted before dispatch.
- Customer service: info@tonesfashion.com
- The policy also contains international duties/taxes and insurance provisions.

## RETURN AND EXCHANGE
URL: https://www.tonesfashion.com/pages/return-exchange-policy
Status: VERIFIED
- 3-day return/exchange window from delivery.
- Defective/incorrect/damaged products should be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
- First reverse shipment is free; later reverse shipment/pickup charges may be Rs. 100 under the policy.
- Return/exchange request is initiated through the linked Logisy portal.
- Items should be unused with original tags and packaging intact; Order ID and registered mobile number should be displayed on the package.
- Approved store credit is stated as being shared within 5–7 business days after inspection/approval; original-payment refunds are not offered according to the policy.
- An alternative store-credit process is stated as 1–3 working days after completion of return pickup.
- Shipping charges are non-refundable.
- Cancellation is possible only before dispatch.
- Certain discounted/coupon/BOGO/promotional purchases are excluded from returns/exchanges.
- Price Drop purchases are stated as final sale and ineligible for return/exchange/refund/store credit.
